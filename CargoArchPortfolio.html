Empowering Smart Logistics.
Ringkasan Proyek Project Overview

CargoArch AI adalah sistem sortasi dan penataan muatan berbasis Edge-AI yang mengintegrasikan Computer Vision, 3D Bin Packing, serta sensor LiDAR & load-cell. Dirancang untuk mengoptimalkan efisiensi penataan kontainer (utilisasi volume ≥85%) berdasarkan rute logistik aktual secara real-time.

CargoArch AI is an Edge-AI based smart sortation and loading system integrating Computer Vision, 3D Bin Packing, and LiDAR & load-cell sensors. Designed to optimize container volume utilization (target ≥85%) based on actual logistic routes in real-time.
